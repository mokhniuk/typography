The International System of Units (SI), 9th edition (SI Brochure) ↗
bipm-si-brochure-9		 Bureau International des Poids et Mesures (BIPM) Standard The definitive international reference on the SI, in force since 2019. Section 5.4 sets how the value of a quantity and a number are written. It is a metrology standard: it covers unit symbols and digit grouping, not general prose typography, and it does not say whether a space is nonbreaking. A space always separates a number from its unit symbol, so "30.2 °C" and not "30.2°C"; the only exception is the plane-angle symbols for degree, minute and second, which take no space. Digits may be grouped in threes by a space, never by commas or dots, and a four-digit number is not split. number.group

UAX #14: Unicode Line Breaking Algorithm, revision 57 (Unicode 18.0.0, 2026-09-01) ↗
uax14		 Unicode Consortium (editor Robin Leroy) Standard The standard that decides where a line may break. It is the mechanism behind every no-break rule here: it says what a no-break space does, not which words to bind. U+00A0 NO-BREAK SPACE is class GL and prohibits a break on either side, and is the preferred way to keep two words on a line (a title and a name); U+202F is the same but narrow; the horizontal ellipsis is class IN, so breaks between consecutive ellipses are never allowed. ellipsis

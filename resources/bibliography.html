cldr-numbers		 Unicode Consortium (CLDR project) Standard How each locale formats percentages and currency amounts, including the space before the sign. UAX #14: Unicode Line Breaking Algorithm, revision 57 (Unicode 18.0.0, 2026-09-01) ↗
uax14		 Unicode Consortium (editor Robin Leroy) Standard The standard that decides where a line may break. It is the mechanism behind every no-break rule here: it says what a no-break space does, not which words to bind. U+00A0 NO-BREAK SPACE is class GL and prohibits a break on either side, and is the preferred way to keep two words on a line (a title and a name); U+202F is the same but narrow; the horizontal ellipsis is class IN, so breaks between consecutive ellipses are never allowed. ellipsis

Practical Typography: Hyphens and dashes ↗
butterick-dashes		 Matthew Butterick (the page itself names no author) Style guide An English-language typography handbook for working writers. Covers hyphen, en dash, em dash and minus; written for English text, so it says nothing about German or Ukrainian dash use. The em dash marks a break within a sentence; em and en dashes are normally set without spaces, and adding a space on each side is acceptable when they look crushed; the en dash is for ranges of values. dash.sentence en

Practical Typography: Ellipses ↗
butterick-ellipsis		 Matthew Butterick (the page itself names no author) Style guide The handbook on the ellipsis: the single character, spaced as the writer prefers, with a no-break space where text is on one side only. Use the ellipsis character, not three periods with or without spaces; put a no-break space on a side that has no text so the ellipsis stays with its text; do not let the dots split across lines. ellipsis en Summary only, check by hand

Practical Typography: Trademark and copyright symbols ↗
butterick-legal		 Matthew Butterick (the page itself names no author) Style guide The handbook on ©, ® and ™: the real characters, where they sit, and a warning that autocorrecting (c) damages text such as "Section 12(c)". Replace (c), (R) and (TM) with ©, ® and ™; no space before ™ or ®; a no-break space between © and the year; do not autocorrect (c) inside citations such as "Section 12(c)". symbol.legal en

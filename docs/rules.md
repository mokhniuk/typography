# Rules

The engine has 21 rules, 20 on by default (the rest are off until named in `enable`). `typographic` runs them in the order below, each on the output of the one before. An id can be switched off with `disable` (see [api.md](api.md)); a group name switches off every rule that starts with it (`space` is every `space.*` rule, `dash` every `dash.*`, `symbol` every `symbol.*`).

This file is generated by `bun run docs:rules` from `RULES` in `src/core/rules.ts` and from the examples in `scripts/gen-rules-doc.ts`. Do not edit it by hand: `src/docs.test.ts` fails when it is out of date.

Each example runs the engine with every rule except the one shown switched off, so it shows what that rule does and nothing else. In a full run the other rules also act (an English `"` is turned into a quote by `quote` before anything else sees it). The text of each example is plain; a no-break space is shown as `~` (the character U+00A0 in the real output; an ordinary space stays a space). Text in a code span, a web address and an email are never changed by any rule.

## `quote`

Quotation marks by language, register and nesting level, from any source style

```text
(en)
before  He said "stone" and left.
after   He said «stone» and left.
```

Display register, the default.

```text
(en)
before  He said "we said 'no' twice" and left.
after   He said «we said „no“ twice» and left.
```

Nesting level two. Source marks may be straight, curly or guillemets.

```text
(en, literary)
before  He said "we said 'no' twice" and left.
after   He said ‘we said “no” twice’ and left.
```

British English in the literary register.

```text
(de)
before  Er sagte "Sie rief 'Haus' laut" und ging.
after   Er sagte »Sie rief ›Haus‹ laut« und ging.
```

## `apostrophe`

A straight ' in a word or before a year becomes ’

```text
(en)
before  It's Maria's turn, don't wait.
after   It’s Maria’s turn, don’t wait.
```

Inside a word.

```text
(en)
before  The '90s were loud.
after   The ’90s were loud.
```

Before a year.

## `ellipsis`

... becomes …

```text
(en)
before  Wait... what?
after   Wait… what?
```

## `arrow`

-> and <- become → and ←

```text
(en)
before  Read more ->
after   Read more →
```

```text
(en)
before  <- Back
after   ← Back
```

## `symbol.legal`

(c) before a year, (C), (R) and (TM) become © ® ™

```text
(en)
before  (c) 2026 Studio (R) (TM)
after   © 2026 Studio ® ™
```

(c) needs a year after it; (C) does not.

## `dash.dialogue`

A Ukrainian dialogue line (literary register) opens with an em dash and a no-break space

```text
(uk, literary)
before  - Привіт, - сказав він.
after   —~Привіт, - сказав він.
```

Only the dash that opens the paragraph; the second one belongs to dash.sentence.

## `number.group`

Digits of an integer of five or more are grouped in threes with the language separator (1,000,000; 1 000 000)

Off by default: switch it on with `enable` (`--enable` on the command line).

```text
(en)
before  We sold 1234567 copies in 2026.
after   We sold 1,234,567 copies in 2026.
```

A run of five or more digits; years are left.

```text
(de)
before  Wir verkauften 1234567 Exemplare, Pi ist 3,14159265.
after   Wir verkauften 1~234~567 Exemplare, Pi ist 3,14159265.
```

A no-break space; the fraction is left alone.

```text
(uk)
before  Продано 12345 примірників, код v1000000.
after   Продано 12~345 примірників, код v1000000.
```

A name glued to digits is left.

## `dash.range`

A hyphen between two numbers (21-26, 10:00-12:00) becomes an unspaced en dash

```text
(en)
before  Open 21-26 September, 10:00-12:00.
after   Open 21–26 September, 10:00–12:00.
```

```text
(en)
before  Pages 5 - 9
after   Pages 5–9
```

Spaced, too.

## `dash.sentence`

A spaced hyphen, dash or double hyphen becomes a spaced dash (em; en in German and literary British English), no-break before it

```text
(en)
before  Wait - what happened -- really?
after   Wait~— what happened~— really?
```

An em dash in English.

```text
(de)
before  Warte - was ist passiert?
after   Warte~– was ist passiert?
```

A spaced en dash in German.

```text
(en, literary)
before  Wait - what happened?
after   Wait~– what happened?
```

A spaced en dash in British English, literary register.

## `symbol.minus`

A hyphen before a digit (-5) becomes a minus sign (−5)

```text
(en)
before  It was -5 degrees (-3 inside).
after   It was −5 degrees (−3 inside).
```

## `symbol.multiply`

The letter x between numbers (1920x1080, 3 x 4) becomes ×

```text
(en)
before  A 1920x1080 screen, a 3 x 4 grid.
after   A 1920×1080 screen, a 3~× 4 grid.
```

## `space.collapse`

A run of spaces becomes one

```text
(en)
before  Two   spaces,    four.
after   Two spaces, four.
```

## `space.title`

No-break space after Prof., Dr., Mr. …

```text
(en)
before  Prof. Dr. Oksana opens.
after   Prof.~Dr.~Oksana opens.
```

## `space.initial`

No-break space after an initial (K. Słoboda)

```text
(en)
before  Written by K. Słoboda.
after   Written by K.~Słoboda.
```

## `space.abbreviation`

Parts of an abbreviation stay together (z. B.), and Nr., ul., S. stay with what follows

```text
(de)
before  z. B. Nr. 5 auf S. 12
after   z.~B. Nr.~5 auf S.~12
```

```text
(uk)
before  вул. Хрещатик, т. д.
after   вул.~Хрещатик, т.~д.
```

```text
(en)
before  See Fig. 2 and p. 14.
after   See Fig.~2 and p.~14.
```

## `space.number`

No-break space between a number and a short word or unit after it, up to nine letters (21 September, 5 %)

```text
(en)
before  On 21 September, 15 people paid 5 %.
after   On 21~September, 15~people paid 5~%.
```

## `space.percent`

German and French: a no-break space between a number and % or € (5 %, 49 €)

```text
(de)
before  5% and 49€
after   5~% and 49~€
```

The rule does nothing in other languages.

```text
(fr)
before  5% and 49€
after   5~% and 49~€
```

## `space.association`

No-break space before e.V.

```text
(de)
before  Kulturverein e.V.
after   Kulturverein~e.V.
```

## `space.copyright`

No-break space after ©

```text
(en)
before  © 2026 Studio
after   ©~2026 Studio
```

## `space.orphan`

No-break space after a one-letter word the language must not strand at a line end

```text
(en)
before  A cat and I
after   A~cat and I
```

```text
(uk)
before  Він і я в домі
after   Він і~я в~домі
```

```text
(pl)
before  Idę w las i z nim
after   Idę w~las i~z~nim
```

## `space.arrow`

No-break space before → and after ←

```text
(en)
before  Read more →
after   Read more~→
```

```text
(en)
before  ← Back
after   ←~Back
```

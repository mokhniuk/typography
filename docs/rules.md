# Rules

The engine has 34 rules, 24 on by default (the rest are off until named in `enable`). `typographic` runs them in the order below, each on the output of the one before. An id can be switched off with `disable` (see [api.md](api.md)); a group name switches off every rule that starts with it (`space` is every `space.*` rule, `dash` every `dash.*`, `symbol` every `symbol.*`).

This file is generated by `bun run docs:rules` from `RULES` in `src/core/rules.ts` and from the examples in `scripts/gen-rules-doc.ts`. Do not edit it by hand: `src/docs.test.ts` fails when it is out of date.

Each example runs the engine with every rule except the one shown switched off, so it shows what that rule does and nothing else. In a full run the other rules also act (an English `"` is turned into a quote by `quote` before anything else sees it). The text of each example is plain; a no-break space is shown as `~` (the character U+00A0 in the real output) and a narrow no-break space as `^` (U+202F); an ordinary space stays a space. Text in a code span, a web address and an email are never changed by any rule.

## Categories

Each rule has a category that says what it is for; the id names the mechanism and is what `disable` and `enable` take. A category is a label only: it is not a group name for `disable`.

- `tradition`: the conventions of setting prose: quotation marks, the apostrophe, dashes, the ellipsis, punctuation and the spaces around it, and what must not be split at a line end
- `symbols`: signs typed as ASCII stand-ins that have a character of their own: arrows, ©, ®, ™
- `math`: signs of arithmetic and logic: minus, ×, ±, fractions, ⇒
- `data`: numbers with what they measure: digit groups, ranges, units, percentages and currencies, dates

## `quote`

Quotation marks by language, register and nesting level, from any source style

Category: `tradition`.

Why: [sources.md](../resources/sources.md#quote), `disputed`.

Characters it can insert, in any language: `“` `”` `‘` `’` `«` `»` `„` `‚` `›` `‹` `~`. A font that lacks one of them is handled by `avoid` (see [api.md](api.md#avoid)).

```text
(en)
before  He said "stone" and left.
after   He said “stone” and left.
```

Display register, the default.

```text
(en)
before  He said "we said 'no' twice" and left.
after   He said “we said ‘no’ twice” and left.
```

Nesting level two. Source marks may be straight, curly or guillemets.

```text
(en, literary)
before  He said "we said 'no' twice" and left.
after   He said ‘we said “no” twice’ and left.
```

British English in the literary register.

```text
(en, quoteStyle guillemets)
before  He said "we said 'no' twice" and left.
after   He said «we said „no“ twice» and left.
```

The house style, in any language and register.

```text
(de)
before  Er sagte "Sie rief 'Haus' laut" und ging.
after   Er sagte »Sie rief ›Haus‹ laut« und ging.
```

```text

```

A paragraph opening with a dash is a line of dialogue, in languages that write dialogue that way: their dash and a no-break space (Ukrainian — Привіт)

Languages with `dialogueDash`: Ukrainian, Polish, French, Czech, Slovak, Swedish, Romanian, Hungarian, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Bulgarian, Croatian, Slovenian, Lithuanian, Danish and Finnish.

## `phone.bind`

An international phone number (+ or 00, at least seven digits) is kept whole: no-break spaces between its groups and non-breaking hyphens inside it, with the digits as typed

Category: `data`.

Why: [sources.md](../resources/sources.md#phonebind), `general-knowledge`.

Off by default: switch it on with `enable` (`--enable` on the command line).

Characters it can insert, in any language: `~` `‑`. A font that lacks one of them is handled by `avoid` (see [api.md](api.md#avoid)).

```text
(en)
before  Call +380 44 123 45 67 or +1 (555) 123-4567
after   Call +380~44~123~45~67 or +1~(555)~123‑4567
```

Off by default. The digits are not reformatted; the hyphen becomes U+2011, which does not break.

```text
(en)
before  Not 555-1234, not 2026-10-20, not DE89 3704 0044 0532 0130 00
after   Not 555-1234, not 2026-10-20, not DE89 3704 0044 0532 0130 00
```

A local number, a date and an IBAN are left alone: the number must open with + or 00 and hold seven to fifteen digits.

## `space.short-word`

In languages whose pack lists shortWords: a short word of two or three letters stays with the word after it (to~the shop); never more than two words in a row

Languages with `shortWords`: English, Ukrainian, Polish, Czech and Slovak.

Category: `tradition`.

Why: [sources.md](../resources/sources.md#spaceshort-word), `general-knowledge`.

Characters it can insert, in any language: `~`. A font that lacks one of them is handled by `avoid` (see [api.md](api.md#avoid)).

```text
(en)
before  Welcome to the city of light
after   Welcome to~the city of~light
```

On by default since 0.14.0, where it was an opt-in rule for titles. The sources read (PWN Poradnia, the ÚJČ advice service) say such a word MAY stay at a line end in body text and must not in a title or heading; this engine is for display text, where a line that ends on "to" reads as a stumble, so the rule runs unless you switch it off (`disable: ['space.short-word']`) for long body text in a narrow column. Never more than two words in a row, so a chain cannot grow into a line; only the words a pack lists, and five packs have a list (cs, en, pl, sk, uk).

```text
(pl)
before  Zapraszamy do Gdańska
after   Zapraszamy do~Gdańska
```

```text
(uk)
before  Ласкаво просимо до міста
after   Ласкаво просимо до~міста
```

Characters it can insert, in any language: `~`. A font that lacks one of them is handled by `avoid` (see [api.md](api.md#avoid)).

```text
(en)
before  Read more →
after   Read more~→
```

```text
(en)
before  ← Back
after   ←~Back
```

## `space.trailing`

Spaces and tabs at the end of a line are removed

Category: `tradition`.

Why: [sources.md](../resources/sources.md#spacetrailing), `general-knowledge`.

Off by default: switch it on with `enable` (`--enable` on the command line).

```text
(en)
before  First line   
Second line	
Third
after   First line
Second line
Third
```

Spaces and tabs at the end of a line; the lines stay.

```text
(en)
before  One trailing space 
after   One trailing space
```

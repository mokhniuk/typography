## Quotation

| Register | Levels, outermost first |
|---|---|
| display | `«~…~»`, `“…”` |
| literary | as display |

A quotation nested deeper than the levels listed starts over from the first.

An apostrophe before a dropped-letter word is not a quotation mark: `'em`, `'til`, `'till`, `'cause`, `'bout`, `'round`, `'tis`, `'twas`, `'cos`, `'n'`.

## Spaces

- One-letter words kept with the next word: none.
- A no-break space between a number and % or €: yes.
- Abbreviations: no list.

## Numbers

Groups of three are separated by `~`, the decimal mark is `,`.

## Notes

A no-break space is set inside the guillemets. Digits are grouped with a no-break space (number.group), as the Interinstitutional Style Guide sets for all languages; the decimal mark is a comma.

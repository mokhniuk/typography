## Quotation

| Register | Levels, outermost first |
|---|---|
| display | `«…»`, `„…“`, `‚…‘` |
| literary | as display |

A quotation nested deeper than the levels listed starts over from the first.

An apostrophe before a dropped-letter word is not a quotation mark: `'em`, `'til`, `'till`, `'cause`, `'bout`, `'round`, `'tis`, `'twas`, `'cos`, `'n'`.

## Spaces

- One-letter words kept with the next word: а А в В і І ї Ї й Й з З у У о О.
- Short words kept with the next word (`space.short-word`, on by default; the pack's own list): `до` `на` `за` `по` `із` `від` `під` `над` `при` `без` `для` `та` `що`.
- A no-break space between a number and %: no.
- A no-break space between a number and € after it: no.
- The space before `:` and before `; ! ?` (`space.high-punctuation`; the opt-in `space.before-punctuation` keeps it): none.
- A bare temperature without the degree sign (`20 C`) read as one (`symbol.degree`, opt-in): no.

## Numbers

## Notes

The literary register keeps the display quotes; the dialogue dash (an em dash and a no-break space) is set in both registers. Digits are grouped with a no-break space (number.group), as the Interinstitutional Style Guide sets for all languages; the decimal mark is a comma. Dialogue: a paragraph of direct speech opens with тире (Український правопис § 125, the 1993 rules as published on pravopys.net; the 2019 edition could not be read).

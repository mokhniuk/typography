## Notes

Quotation: the ISG (4.2.3, 6.4 and 10.1.6) and the Publications Office table write `“…”` (U+201C, U+201D, both marks at the top, as in English) at every level, so a quotation inside a quotation is `“…“…”…”` here. This is not the German-shaped `„…“` that a Latvian reader may expect from other sources: `„…“` was suggested to this pack and was not found in the ISG, so it is not set; no Latviešu valodas aģentūra or Valsts valodas centrs page was read, so nothing says whether `„…“` is also accepted by the national authority. The ISG puts single quotes `‘…’` around translations of words, explanations of meaning and plant variety names (4.2.3, 10.1.6). The engine cannot tell such a use from a nested quotation, so a top-level `‘…’` is not kept (the nesting level decides); this is not implemented. Quotes of the third level are set as `“…”` as well. Dash: ISG 10.1.7 sets a space on each side of the dash; its examples ("brīdinājuma zīme" – zīme, ila – `‘daba’`) use the en dash, and 4.2.3 reserves the em dash for lists and empty table cells, so sentenceDash is U+2013. Not implemented, not verified: the ISG gives no list of one-letter words (k, s, v, z, a, i, o, u) to keep off the end of a line, so there are no orphans; elisions: none read; the abbreviation list is only those the ISG itself writes with a fixed space in 10.7 (u. c., u. tml., t. i., š. g., v. i., z. v., P. S.) and Nr.; lpp. (7.–13. lpp.) follows a number and is already kept with it by the number rule; the ISG writes a time range with full stops and an en dash (9.00–18.30, 10.1.7), and the range rule sets it (9.00-18.30 becomes 9.00–18.30; 9:00-18:30 too); the ISG shows € only as the code EUR, so the pack sets percentSpace and not currencySpace (49€ stays 49€). Numbers: ISG 6.5 gives a comma for decimals and a fixed space between groups of three; the engine groups from five digits, as it does in every language.

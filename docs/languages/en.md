## Quotation

| Register | Levels, outermost first |
|---|---|
| display | `“…”`, `‘…’` |
| literary | `‘…’`, `“…”` |

A quotation nested deeper than the levels listed starts over from the first.

An apostrophe before a dropped-letter word is not a quotation mark: `'em`, `'til`, `'till`, `'cause`, `'bout`, `'round`, `'tis`, `'twas`, `'cos`, `'n'`.

## Spaces

- One-letter words kept with the next word: a A I.
- Short words kept with the next word (`space.short-word`, on by default; the pack's own list): `to` `of` `in` `on` `at` `by` `as` `or` `if` `an` `the` `and` `for`.
- A no-break space between a number and %: no.
- A no-break space between a number and € after it: no.

## Notes

Display register uses `“…”` with `‘…’` inside, as CLDR gives English; British quotes (`‘…’` with `“…”` inside) are the literary register; quoteStyle: 'guillemets' sets the former house style, `«…»` with `„…“` inside. Digits of a number of five or more are grouped with a comma (opt-in rule number.group): the EU English Style Guide sets a hard space in running text and allows a comma for web writing, promotional material and press releases, which is what this engine is for. The separator is the pack field numbers.group (a no-break space is the EU form).

## Spaces

- One-letter words kept with the next word: none.
- Short words kept with the next word (`space.short-word`, on by default; the pack's own list): none.
- A no-break space between a number and %: yes.
- A no-break space between a number and € after it: yes.
- The space before `:` and before `; ! ?` (`space.high-punctuation`; the opt-in `space.before-punctuation` keeps it): none.
- A bare temperature without the degree sign (`20 C`) read as one (`symbol.degree`, opt-in): no.

## Notes

Dialogue: the ajatusviiva is used as a dialogue dash instead of quotes, especially in interviews and fiction (Kielitoimiston ohjepankki, "Lainausmerkit ja vuorosanaviiva"); the attribution follows a comma. Quotes: `”…”` (ISG 10.4.5 "kokolainausmerkit"; the ISG shows `”…”` for all levels in 4.2.3 and uses `’…’` only for the definitions of legal acts, 3.6). The inner level `’…’` is from Kotus, not from the ISG. The same mark opens and closes, so a quotation is read by where each mark stands, not by pairing. The alternative `»…»` (also used in Finnish) is not implemented, and the ISG and Kotus say nothing about a third level. Dash: the ISG 6.4 table spaces a short dash (Alt 0150) and 10.4.8 keeps the long dash for list dashes only, so a break in the sentence is a spaced en dash; 4.2.3 notes that the long list dash of EU texts departs from the Finnish standard language, which uses the shorter dash. Numbers: a comma and a "ohuke" (thin fixed space) between groups of three (ISG 6.5, 10.2.1); the pack uses the ordinary no-break space. Abbreviations: those the ISG lists in 10.7.1 as examples and that end with a period (p., s., v., mrd.), and two it lists without a period that come before a number, klo and nro (the bare list: "klo 17" and "nro 5" are kept together; the ISG uses a fixed space after nro and s. in 4.2.3). The ISG also lists hra, rva, tsto without a period; they come before a name or stand alone, so they are not bound. A bare abbreviation is matched in lower case only (Klo at the start of a sentence is left alone). Not implemented, not verified: one-letter words (the ISG gives no list), the hyphen rules of 10.4.7, the colon before case endings (p:ssa), dates (1.4.–30.4.), and Kotus guidance beyond the two pages named in the sources.

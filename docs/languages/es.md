## Notes

Quotation: «…» then “…” then ‘…’ is the order of the Libro de estilo interinstitucional (10.1.12), which it takes from the RAE; the Mexican Academy recommends the reverse for the Americas (“…‘…«…»…’…”), and “…” first is common in Latin America. The option quoteStyle: 'us' gives “…” with ‘…’ inside. Not implemented, not verified: the double raya of 10.1.8 is written attached to the text it encloses (—inciso—) and spaced from the text outside, but the engine sets a spaced dash on both sides (x — y), so a Spanish aside comes out spaced inside; dialogue lines (—Hola) are left alone, since no dialogue rule was read; the ISG gives no list of one-letter words to keep off the end of a line, so there are no orphans; the ISG shows the € symbol only in graphics and press releases (7.3.3) and puts a fixed space before the code EUR, so the space before € here follows the code. Numbers: ISG 6.5 prescribes a comma and a fixed space; secondary articles report that the 2005 RAE dictionary of doubts and Latin American usage differ on the % space and on the decimal point; none of that was read at its source. The abbreviation lists are only those the ISG itself gives as examples of a non-split abbreviation (10.4 and the fixed-space list in 4.2.3); the ordinals n.º and D.ª are not covered.
